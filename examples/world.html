World map

The arcade world map (world_map.jpg) rebuilt only from catalogue sprites as one static image: Lake Joy and Lake Byte joined through a swamp, the river running in from the north and out to the south, trampled-grass trails, a sand beach that turns into a rocky desert, forests, mountains and a few monsters. Towns, the market and the castle are not in the library and are left out; a single tent marks Pixel Town.

Data and renderer: world.js; agent spec: WORLD-SPEC.md. Drawn at 2× the reference image (2752 × 1536 px).
