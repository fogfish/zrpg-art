Ponds

Small water bodies (≈100–200 px across) built from a blob, a tile grid, or a blob joined to a brook.

Each card switches between grass, rocky and sand shores. New variation re-rolls the noise seed. Debug bands marks the waterline (cyan), the outer shore edge (orange), the bank face (magenta) and decor anchors (red). Agent spec: ponds.md.
