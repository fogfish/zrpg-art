Sand, rock and grass terrain

Mesas, ground patches, pits and rings are 9-slice tilesets: corners, edges that repeat along each side, and a fill that repeats inside, all cut on the 32 px grid. Change the centre size of any card to see the same pieces build a bigger or smaller area. Tilesets marked hole have no fill, so the ground below shows through.

Data: catalog.tilesets. Renderer: terrain.js (ZRPGTerrain.load / draw / size).
