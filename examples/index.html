Ponds, lakes and rivers

The ZRPG catalogue has no shore or edge autotiles, only a seamless animated water texture (320×128, 21 frames). Water that is placed tile by tile therefore shows hard square edges. These examples paint water and shore in distance bands around a water mask instead, then scatter sprites by distance to the waterline.

Pages are for people. Agents should read WATER-SPEC.md for the algorithm and ponds.md, lakes.md and rivers.md for exact recipes. The reference renderer is water.js.

What makes it look natural

1. Shape is a mask, not tilesBlobs with harmonic wobble, rivers as discs swept along a spline, or a tile grid rounded by blur and threshold.
2. Signed distance SEvery pixel knows how far it is from the waterline. Every band and every decoration is chosen by S.
3. Bands vary in widthLow-frequency noise widens and narrows the shore. High-frequency noise roughens both of its edges.
4. Bank face (3/4 view)On north banks the ground drops into the water as a 1–6 px earth or stone face with a shadow below it. South banks get a light rim instead.
5. Shallows and foamTwo stepped, dithered shallow tints plus a broken foam line that laps in and out over 4 phases.
6. Decor straddles the edgeBoulders, reeds and stones sit across the waterline with a ripple ring. This hides the seam between water and land.
